Common questions

How do I apply for a home?

Open any home in the Available rentals section and select Apply on the listing page. You can apply from your phone or computer, and screening is handled online through TurboTenant.

How do you screen applicants?
  • Identity verification
  • Landlord references
  • Credit check
  • Income and employer verification
  • Eviction and background checks
How do I pay rent and request repairs?

Log in to your TurboTenant renter account from the Resident login button to pay rent, fees and deposits online and to submit maintenance requests.

What if something breaks at night?

Our maintenance team takes urgent requests 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

What happens when my lease is ending?

We reach out well ahead of time with renewal options, and if you need a different size or neighborhood, we’ll help you move within the Sphere network.

Two ways to rent your home.

We manage both, and can help you decide which fits your property.

Mid-term rentals

Flexible stays for traveling professionals, relocating families and people between homes.

  • Furnished or unfurnished options
  • Shorter, flexible lease terms
  • Often higher monthly rent

Long-term rentals

Traditional leases for residents who want to settle in.

  • Steady, predictable income
  • Fewer turnovers
  • Lower day-to-day effort
